## Made in code

Every picture in Dirtbag is drawn in code: the landscapes, the rock, the people, the dog, the cards it draws of your sends. Every sound is made in code too, the music included. There are no sprites and no recordings in it. The type is open-licensed (OFL): Patrick Hand, Patrick Hand SC and Big Shoulders Display.

It asks nothing of the internet: no ads, no accounts, no tracking. It plays offline, and the save stays on the player's device.
