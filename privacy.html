Dirtbag — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · RCJ Labs

The short version: Dirtbag collects nothing, sends nothing, and has no servers. Your game save lives only on your own device. There is no account, no login, and no tracking.

What data the game handles

Dirtbag is a single-player game that runs entirely on your device. The only data it stores is:

  • Your game save — your climber, progress, and settings — kept in your browser/app's local storage on your device. It never leaves your device and is not visible to us or anyone else.
  • Your preferences — sound, text size, and similar options — stored the same way, as part of the save.

What the game does NOT do

  • No accounts, sign-in, or personal information collected.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers.
  • No network requests — the game makes no calls to any server, ours or otherwise.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or file access.
  • No data is sold, shared, or transmitted, because none is collected.

Optional device features

Two optional, on-device features may use standard browser capabilities only when you choose to use them:

  • Copy diagnostics — if you tap the diagnostic-info button in Settings, the game copies a short text summary to your clipboard so you can paste it into a bug report. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Keep screen awake — if you enable it, the game asks the system to keep the screen on while you play. No data is involved.

Children's privacy

Dirtbag does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is nothing to disclose or delete.

Data deletion

Your save is entirely under your control. Clearing the app's data / storage, or uninstalling the app, permanently removes your game save from your device. We hold no copy because we never receive one.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new date.
